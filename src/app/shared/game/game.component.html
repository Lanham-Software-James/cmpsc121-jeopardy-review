Let’s play.

Rename the teams on the scoreboard, then choose a point value. The instructor reads the clue and selects the responding team.

  1. The 30-second timer starts when a clue opens. Pause or restart it as needed; it never scores automatically.
  2. Correct adds the clue’s value and completes the clue.
  3. Incorrect subtracts its value. Another team may try; negative scores are allowed.
  4. Reveal the answer for an explanation. Use No answer / finish clue to complete it without awarding points.

Closing a clue leaves it available. Scores already awarded or deducted stay in place. Undo reverses the most recent score and reopens a clue completed by that score.

The highest score after all {{ game.total }} clues wins; ties share the win. Answers need not be phrased as questions.

Up to six teams can play. Progress lasts while this page stays open and resets on refresh.
